About AD Hospital & Research Centre

Dedicated to Accessible, Patient-First Healthcare in Lower Assam

Located in Borpara, Mayapuri, AD Hospital & Research Centre was established to provide reliable, ethical, and modern medical care to families in Bongaigaon and neighbouring regions.
